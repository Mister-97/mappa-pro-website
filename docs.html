🚀 Getting Started

Setting up your account

Create your Mappa Pro agency and get everything configured in under five minutes.

Create your account

Go to app.mappapro.co and click Sign Up. You'll enter your name, agency name, email, and password. Your account is created as an Owner — the highest access level with full control over your workspace.

First-time setup checklist

1

Name your organization

After signup you'll be prompted to confirm your agency name. This appears across your dashboard and in team invite emails.

2

Connect your first Fanvue account

Go to Accounts → Add Account. You'll be redirected to Fanvue's login page to authorize the connection.

3

Invite your team

Head to Team → Invite Member. Add chatters and managers — they'll receive a setup email automatically.

4

Assign accounts to chatters

Click Assign on any chatter to choose which Fanvue accounts they can access.

5

Configure commission

In Earnings & Payouts, click the ⚙️ gear icon to set your agency's commission rate and base (gross or net).

ℹ️
No credit card required to get started. Connect accounts and explore the platform before upgrading.

🔗 Accounts

Connecting Fanvue accounts

Mappa Pro connects via Fanvue's official OAuth 2.0 API. Your creator passwords are never stored — only encrypted tokens.

How it works

Mappa Pro uses Fanvue's official OAuth authorization flow — the same secure method used by major platforms. When you connect an account, you're redirected to Fanvue's official login page. After you authorize, Fanvue sends a token back which we encrypt and store securely. Your password never touches our servers.

✅
You never share the creator's Fanvue password with Mappa Pro. Authorization happens entirely on Fanvue's own page.

Connecting an account

1

Go to Accounts

Click Accounts in the left sidebar.

2

Click "Add Account"

You'll be redirected to Fanvue's OAuth login page.

3

Log in on Fanvue

Enter the creator's Fanvue credentials and approve the connection.

4

Account appears in dashboard

You're redirected back and the account starts syncing revenue and fan data immediately.

Account health monitoring

The Accounts tab shows a System Health card per account with:

  • Token status (valid / expired)
  • Last sync timestamp
  • Unread message count
  • Follow-up needed count
  • Pending outbound messages

Reconnecting after expiry

If a Fanvue account's password changes, the token is invalidated. You'll see a red reconnect banner at the top of the app. Click Reconnect on the affected account to go through the OAuth flow again.

⚠️
If a creator changes their Fanvue password, you'll need to reconnect their account. Existing data is preserved.

Pending invites

The Accounts tab also shows Pending Invites — Fanvue account invites you've sent but that haven't been accepted yet. You can cancel or track expiry dates from this table.

First-time onboarding guide

A quick reference for everything new users need to know on day one.

For owners

  • Sign up → name your agency → connect Fanvue accounts → invite team → assign → set commission
  • Use the Overview tab as your home base — it shows total revenue, subscriber counts, and top spenders across all accounts
  • Use Earnings & Payouts to see per-chatter performance and configure commission rates

  • Log in and you'll land on the Inbox — your assigned accounts' conversations are already here
  • Click the clock icon in the sidebar to clock in and start tracking your shift
  • Reply to fans, send PPV, and use scripts/vault to work efficiently
  • Clock out when your shift ends — your KPIs are saved automatically

For managers

  • You have access to the inbox, analytics, and team management for your assigned accounts
  • Use the Earnings & Payouts tab to monitor chatter performance across your team
  • You can invite and assign new chatters without needing owner approval
✅

📥 Inbox

Unified inbox

All fan conversations across every connected Fanvue account — in one place, with no tab-switching.

How it works

The inbox syncs all fan conversations from every account assigned to you. Messages appear sorted by most recent. Each thread shows the fan's name, avatar, which creator account it belongs to, the last message, and an unread indicator.

Filtering & sorting

Use the controls at the top of the inbox to:

  • Sort by: Most recent, Oldest first, Alphabetical, Most spent, Least spent
  • Filter by: All conversations, Mine (your assigned only), or a saved custom filter
  • Search by fan name across all conversations
  • Save filter presets — create named rule sets and apply them with one click

The conversation view

Inside a conversation you can see the full message thread with timestamps and PPV indicators. The right panel shows the fan's profile — spend history, subscription status, and notes — without leaving the conversation.

The fan detail panel

The right sidebar inside every conversation shows:

  • Fan avatar, display name, and handle
  • Lifetime spend and last purchase date
  • Total PPV revenue and average PPV price
  • Total tips and average tip amount
  • Subscription status, cost, duration, and auto-renewal
  • PPV purchase history (full list in modal)
  • Custom nickname and saved notes
  • Follow-up flag
💡
The fan detail panel gives chatters the context they need to personalize messages and maximize revenue — without leaving the inbox.

Scripts are reusable message templates saved per Fanvue account. They let chatters send consistent, on-brand messages without writing from scratch every time. Templates are organized into folders for easy browsing.

Using a script in the inbox

3

Browse and select

4

Edit if needed and send

Personalize before sending, or send as-is.

Managing scripts

Owners and managers can create, edit, and delete scripts. Scripts are scoped per account, so each creator can have their own voice and content library.

4

⏱️ Team

Chatter clock-in & performance

Track every shift, message, and dollar your chatters generate — automatically.

Clocking in and out

Chatters click the clock icon in the sidebar to start and end shifts. The system records:

  • Shift duration — total minutes worked per session
  • Messages sent — every message logged automatically in the background
  • PPV sent & revenue — count and dollar value of PPV content sent this shift
  • Fans messaged — unique fan count per shift
  • Average response time — seconds to first reply on each inbound message
💡
If a chatter forgets to clock out, the system automatically closes the open session when they clock in for their next shift.

Performance cards (owners/managers)

In Earnings & Payouts, each chatter gets a card with two groups:

  • Revenue: Gross · Net · Commission · PPV Revenue
  • Activity: PPV Sent · Hours Worked · Shifts · Messages Sent

A Live / Offline badge shows whether the chatter is currently clocked in.

Chatter's own view

Chatters can see their personal KPIs in the Earnings & Payouts tab — their own stats only. No revenue figures or other team members' data is visible to them.

Filtering by period

Performance stats can be filtered by: 7 days, 30 days, this month, this year, or all time.

💸 Team

Commission tracking

Set a rate once, and Mappa Pro auto-calculates every chatter's commission from real Fanvue revenue.

Configuring your commission rate

In Earnings & Payouts, click the ⚙️ gear icon on the Commission card:

  • Base toggle — choose Gross (before Fanvue's platform fee) or Net (after fee)
  • Rate slider — 0% to 100%

Settings are saved per-device and apply immediately to all calculations on the screen.

How commission is calculated

For each chatter: Commission = PPV Revenue (gross or net) × Rate

The result is shown on their performance card and updates live when you adjust the rate or change the period.

ℹ️
Payout balance (available + pending) shows "Coming soon" — Fanvue does not currently expose wallet balance through their public API. This will update automatically when Fanvue makes it available.

📋 Fan Intelligence

Nicknames, notes & follow-ups

Add context to any fan — private nicknames, free-form notes, and follow-up flags to ensure no lead goes cold.

Custom nicknames

In the fan detail panel inside any conversation, you can set a custom nickname for the fan. This is a private label visible only to your team — not to the fan. It's useful for remembering who's who without relying on Fanvue display names.

Notes

The Notes field in the fan panel is a free-text area for anything your team needs to remember about that fan — their preferences, what content they like, when they last bought PPV, etc. Notes auto-save as you type.

Follow-up flags

Mark any fan with a follow-up flag to surface them in your filter presets. This ensures high-priority fans don't get lost in a full inbox. You can create inbox filter presets that show only flagged conversations.

💡
Nicknames and notes are visible to all chatters assigned to that account — so your whole team stays on the same page about each fan.

🔒 Security

Security & password

Change your password and understand how Mappa Pro keeps your data and Fanvue connections secure.

Changing your password

1

Settings → Security

Click ⚙️ in the nav and select the Security tab.

2

Enter current and new password

Fill in your current password and a new one (min 8 characters).

3

Save

Click Update Password. You stay logged in; other active sessions are invalidated.

How we protect your data

  • Secure Fanvue connection — Account connections use Fanvue's official authorization flow. Your creator passwords never reach Mappa Pro servers.
  • Encrypted token storage — All Fanvue access tokens are encrypted before being stored.
  • Authenticated requests — Every request between your browser and our backend is authenticated and verified.
  • Organization-level data isolation — Users can only access data belonging to their own organization.
  • Server-side role enforcement — Permissions are enforced on the server. Access cannot be escalated from the browser.
✅
Use a strong, unique password for your Mappa Pro account. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately.
